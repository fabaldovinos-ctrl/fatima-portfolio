TRAINING DECK

Design for Non-Designers

A visual communication training deck created to teach basic design principles in a clear, approachable, and organized way.

PROJECT PURPOSE

Making design principles easier to understand

This training deck was created to explain design fundamentals to learners without formal design training. It focuses on clarity, visual hierarchy, moodboards, layout choices, and practical decision making.

The goal was to create a learning resource that feels calm, structured, and visually polished while still being easy to understand.
