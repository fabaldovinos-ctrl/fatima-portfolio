ABOUT ME

Creating learning experiences that feel clear, human, and meaningful.

My work is rooted in the belief that learning should feel practical, accessible, and empowering. After years of supporting people through education, coaching, onboarding, and workplace training, I became passionate about designing experiences that help people feel confident in what they do.

With a background in psychology, bilingual education, workforce development, and employee support, I bring a human centered perspective to instructional design with a focus on clarity, empathy, and real world impact.

BEHIND THE WORK
“Behind every training experience is a desire to help people feel capable, confident, and supported.”

Whether I’m simplifying complex processes, designing onboarding experiences, or creating bilingual training materials, my goal is always the same: helping people feel supported while they learn.

WHAT GUIDES MY WORK

Learning Design Strengths

Bilingual Learning Design

Creating accessible learning experiences in both English and Spanish with clarity, empathy, and cultural awareness.

Human Centered Training

Designing practical learning experiences that support confidence, engagement, and real workplace application.

Structured Learning Systems

Building onboarding journeys, training resources, and scalable systems that help organizations support people more effectively.

A personal note

Education has always represented opportunity, dignity, and transformation in my life. That belief continues to shape how I approach learning design today with curiosity, compassion, and a genuine desire to help people succeed.
