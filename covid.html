CASE STUDY

Understanding COVID for Children: A Learning Guide

An educational resource created to help employees explain COVID to their children in a clear, calm, and age appropriate way during a time of uncertainty.

The Purpose

During the COVID pandemic, the Human Resources team at Disal wanted employees to feel supported, especially those who continued working on site. One way to provide support was by creating resources they could use with their families.

This learning guide was designed to help parents explain COVID to their children in a way that felt informative, calm, and emotionally safe.

My Approach

I focused on translating complex health information into simple, accessible language. The tone was intentionally warm and reassuring, helping children understand what was happening without creating fear.

The content combined clear explanations with visual storytelling to make the experience engaging and easier to follow.

Why it Matters

This project shows my ability to adapt complex information for a specific audience, combining educational design with emotional awareness. It reflects how learning can go beyond the workplace and support people in meaningful, human ways.
